Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Exifless handles information when you use the app. Exifless is designed as an offline photo privacy tool. Its core features run locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Exifless does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties. The app does not require an account, login, cloud database, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or internet connection.

2. Photo and Metadata Processing

Exifless can read photos on your device to inspect EXIF metadata, detect metadata risk, find visually similar photos, create cleaned copies, compress images, apply simple image adjustments, and prepare photos for sharing.

Photo files and metadata are processed locally on your device. Exifless does not upload your photos, thumbnails, EXIF metadata, scan results, or edited images to any server.

3. Permissions Used

Exifless requests only the following runtime permission:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Used to read images for privacy scanning, EXIF inspection, duplicate detection, metadata removal, and image export workflows.

Exifless does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, phone, precise location, broad file management, notification, all-apps-list, or internet permissions.

4. Local Storage

Exifless may store app preferences, scan history, duplicate scan results, and basic local statistics in app storage on your device. This information is used only to provide app functionality and is not transmitted off the device.

Cleaned or edited images are saved only when you choose to export or share them. By default, Exifless creates a new output file and does not modify the original photo.

5. Sharing

When you choose to share a cleaned photo, Exifless prepares a local temporary file and opens the Android system share sheet. The app you select in the system share sheet is responsible for its own privacy practices.

6. Third-Party Services

Exifless does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud storage services in this version. The app may use open-source Android libraries for local app functionality, such as image loading, database storage, and metadata processing.

7. Children's Privacy

Exifless is not directed to children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Data generated by Exifless is stored locally on your device. You can delete exported images from your gallery, clear app data through Android system settings, or uninstall the app to remove local app data.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality or legal requirements change. The updated version will show a new "Last updated" date.
